02 / How the science works

See the argument,
not just the answer.

RINet treats a structure as a residue graph: residues are nodes, non-local Cα contacts are edges, and each ranking feature remains visible. It does not infer function from centrality alone. Instead, it asks whether a graph-prioritized perturbation produces a larger biological effect than a structurally matched lower-signal control while expression and fold remain intact.

03 / What is actually new

The missing layer between a structure viewer and an experiment.

Structural biologists already have excellent tools for visualization, sequence analysis, molecular dynamics and energy calculations. RINet does not imitate them. It packages the residue-network question into a reproducible decision workflow and keeps the evidence attached to the recommendation.

VIEWER / DAILY TOOLShows coordinates and lets you choose an analysis.

Essential for expert inspection, but it does not automatically construct a matched experimental contrast or a stopping rule.

RINETShows why one residue outranks another.

Exact score contributions, full ranking, graph embedding, cutoff sensitivity, known-site recovery and a chemistry-aware mutation ladder.

EXPERIMENTAL VALUESeparates site-specific biology from damaged protein.

Candidate and matched lower-signal control are measured beside wild type, with abundance and folding gates before function is interpreted.

Scientific boundaryA static residue graph is not an all-atom potential, evolutionary model, MD trajectory, free-energy calculation or proof of allostery. RINet exposes those missing evidence classes so they can be added rather than silently implied.
