02 / What you get

Upload one PDB.
Know what to test next.

RINet finds residues that stand out in the supplied structure, explains them in biological language, suggests a comparison residue, and turns the result into a practical experiment plan.

04 / Try it now

One PDB in.
An experiment guide out.

Drop a PDB, enter a PDB ID, or run the instant demo. See the 3D surface, what the protein may be useful for, the experiment worth running, comparison controls and a methods-ready record. Deterministic graph theory produces the guidance. No AI model is involved, and your coordinates stay in the browser.
