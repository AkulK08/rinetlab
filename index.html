01 / The product

A supplied structure in.
A falsifiable evidence record out.

Most structure tools end at a colored model or a ranked list. RINet keeps going: compare states, challenge domain boundaries, rebuild residue graphs under alternative definitions, and surface the places where the evidence disagrees.

Every claim retains its coordinates, assumptions, masks, warnings and matched controls. Static graph evidence stays a hypothesis. A geometric interpolation stays a display—not a molecular-dynamics trajectory.

A

State Atlas

Superposition, pairwise RMSD, contact changes and rigid-community motion across 2–32 supplied states.

B

Domain Consensus

Multi-scale PAE partitions, co-assignment, stability and explicit abstention when the inputs do not support a clean answer.

C

Allosteric Consensus

Multiple graph definitions, supplied-state agreement, degree-preserving nulls and matched negative controls.

D

Evidence Ledger

One residue identity across state, uncertainty and graph evidence—with disagreement kept visible.
