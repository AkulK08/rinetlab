Why these candidates differ from one another

To avoid spending the first round on near-duplicate sites, RINet spreads candidates across five provisional structural response patterns. These are hand-set design assumptions—not learned mechanisms, physical energy calculations, or probabilities.

01Long-range graph position

Emphasizes long-range contacts, closeness, and betweenness.

02Direct local contact

Emphasizes ligand, metal, interface, and local-contact geometry.

03Protein-quality sensitivity

Emphasizes the possibility that a mutation lowers abundance or disrupts fold/assembly.

04Packing

Emphasizes contact density, weighted packing, and burial.

05Mixed structural features

Represents sites for which no single displayed feature group dominates.

Method scope

What RINet calculates

From one coordinate model, RINet records typed atom-level contacts, builds a separate Cα contact graph, calculates the displayed residue scores, selects lower-scoring structural controls, and repeats the ranking at 7, 8, and 9 Å cutoffs. The atomic layer explains local geometry; the graph layer describes wider structural position. Neither is treated as an energy calculation or proof of information flow.

INPUTStructure

Author chain IDs, residue numbering, ligands, missing atoms and quality fields remain visible.

CALCULATIONInspect, rank, compare

Typed atomic contacts, graph position, every weighted term, and a matched lower-score control.

EXPERIMENT OUTPUTCandidate + control

Mutation set, protein-quality measurements, functional readout, and interpretation rule.

NOT CALCULATEDEnergy or causality

ΔG, dynamics, evolution, cellular phenotype, and causal mechanism require separate analyses and experimental evidence.

D / SCOPE OF THIS CHECK

Reference check, not general validation

The built-in known sites test whether the ranking recovers established structural anchors in selected examples. It is not a substitute for blinded multi-protein benchmarking against mutational, allosteric, evolutionary or MD datasets.

C / RESULT CLASSIFICATION
Four common outcomes
Function changes; quality holds

Candidate has a function-specific effect relative to the control.

Function and quality both fall

Treat as a stability, expression, or assembly defect first.

Candidate equals control

The experiment does not distinguish the selected site from structural background.

No measurable effect

Check assay sensitivity, then test the next site.

07

Interpretation limits

Read before ordering constructs

Static coordinates

No dynamics, kinetics, conformational ensemble or energetic coupling is inferred from one model.

Geometry is not energy

Typed atom contacts and the Cα graph describe geometry. They are not an all-atom potential, folding model, free-energy calculation, or evidence that information flows along graph edges.

Biological assembly

The supplied coordinate file may contain an asymmetric unit, crystal contacts or an incomplete biological assembly.

Numbering and construct

Author numbering, insertion codes, unresolved residues, tags and assay constructs must be reconciled experimentally.

Evolution absent by default

Conservation is not invented. Use the FASTA/BLAST route or import external evidence before making evolutionary claims.

Validation scope

Known-site recovery and cutoff stability support inspection; they do not establish general predictive performance.
